# hackathon

PS01: ScamShield-Suspicious Message Detector

Domain: Cybersecurity
With the increasing use of digital communication, people frequently receive fraudulent SMS messages, WhatsApp messages and suspicious links pretending to be from banks, delivery services and government organisations. Many individuals struggle to distinguish between genuine and fraudulent messages. Develop a web application that helps users identify potentially fraudulent messages and understand the risks associated with suspicious digital communication.

PS02: MediTrack-Smart Medicine Reminder

Domain: Healthcare

Many individuals, particularly elderly people, often forget their medication schedules, making it difficult to maintain consistent healthcare routines. Develop a simple web application that helps users organise, manage and track their daily medication schedules through an accessible and user-friendly platform.

PS03: GreenBin-Smart Waste Management Assistant

Domain: Environment

Improper waste disposal and a lack of awareness about waste segregation contribute significantly to environmental pollution. Many people struggle to identify recyclable, biodegradable and hazardous waste. Develop a web application that promotes responsible waste management and helps individuals understand proper waste segregation and disposal practices.

PS04: EduBuddy-Student Learning Assistant

Domain: Education

Students often struggle to understand complex academic topics and find learning resources that match their understanding levels. Develop an interactive learning platform that helps students simplify difficult concepts, improve their understanding and make their learning experience more effective through AI-powered assistance.

PS05: Northeast Connect-Community Emergency Assistant

Domain: North East Development & Disaster Management

Several regions across North East India face challenges related to floods, landslides and other natural disasters. During emergencies, communities may struggle to access important safety information and emergency resources. Develop a web-based platform that helps people access essential emergency information, stay informed and connect with relevant assistance during disaster situations.

PS06: KrishiMitra-Smart Farming Assistant

Domain: Agriculture

Farmers often face difficulties in identifying suitable crops, understanding seasonal farming practices and accessing basic agricultural guidance. Develop an intelligent farming assistance platform that provides useful agricultural insights and helps farmers make better decisions based on their local conditions and farming requirements.

PS07: SchemeFinder-Government Scheme Discovery Platform

Domain: Public Services

Many citizens are unaware of government welfare schemes and benefits available to them. Finding relevant schemes and understanding their eligibility requirements can be confusing. Develop a web application that simplifies the discovery of government schemes and helps individuals identify benefits that may be relevant to their circumstances.

PS08: TravelMateNE-North East Tourism Explorer

Domain: Tourism & Culture

North East India is home to diverse cultures, beautiful landscapes and numerous tourist destinations. However, travellers often struggle to discover local attractions, understand travel options and plan affordable trips. Develop a tourism discovery platform that encourages people to explore North East India and makes travel planning easier and more accessible.
